FloraKeys Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Applies to the FloraKeys keyboard for HarmonyOS (com.bulletuae.florakeys)

FloraKeys is a keyboard (input method). Because a keyboard sees what you type, this page explains exactly what it does with that. In short: FloraKeys collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

No internet, no collection

FloraKeys does not request the internet permission and has no network code. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, typed text, usage statistics or device identifiers. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

To work, FloraKeys keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone. Nothing leaves the device:

  • Settings you choose (theme, key height, language, autocorrect and similar).
  • Kept words: words you undo an autocorrection for, so they aren't corrected again. You can clear them in Settings → "Forget kept words".
  • Recent emoji you used.
  • Clipboard history: only text you copy or cut with FloraKeys' own Copy and Cut buttons (up to 20 items plus any you pin). FloraKeys cannot read your system clipboard. You can delete items or clear the history in the clipboard panel.

Text you type is used only in the moment to show suggestions and corrections, using word lists built into the app. It is not stored, except as listed above.

Sensitive fields

Autocorrect, suggestions and learning are switched off in password, email, web address and number fields.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling FloraKeys deletes everything it stored. You can also clear kept words and clipboard history inside the keyboard at any time.

Children

FloraKeys collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
